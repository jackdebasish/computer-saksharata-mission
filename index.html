About Us

A Local Mission for Digital Education in Hirakud

Located at Laxmi Narayan Mandir Complex, Hirakud, we serve learners from Hirakud, Sambalpur and nearby areas with well-structured computer courses, hands-on practice and continuous guidance.

  • Practical-first learning — every student gets individual computer time, not just theory.
  • Affordable fee structure so that no learner is left behind in the digital age.
  • Job-oriented syllabus — Tally, DTP, typing, programming and office automation.
  • Friendly, experienced faculty who guide every student personally.
